Caster-aware digital twin

We are studying how the passive caster affects a differential-drive robot during turns, reversals and docking. The next step is to compare those effects with measurements from the Arche platform.

Status as of 27 November 2026

Simulation preparation is complete

The models, test runs and pilot protocol are ready for the Arche visit. The simulation work gives us repeatable commands and a set of cases to compare later on the robot. The technical visit and operator-assisted pilot are awaiting confirmation.
