06 Research scope

Experiment protocol ↗

Three questions. One bounded study.

RQ1 / Primary

Model the caster.

Does an explicit caster model reproduce measured motion better than an ideal drive model?

Required: matched measured commands, reference motion and a defensible calibration.

RQ2 / Primary

Smooth the motion.

Can a smoother command profile reduce tracking and yaw error without an unacceptable time penalty?

Required: repeated baseline/improved-profile trials on the approved surfaces.

RQ3 / Secondary

Predict the error.

Can recent telemetry predict short-horizon yaw error better than simple baselines?

Required: sufficient independent runs, causal inputs and a held-out evaluation.

Planned bounds: one robot configuration · two surfaces, subject to approval · four maneuver families · one improved profile

24 calibration/development runs + 48 confirmation runs are planned, excluding pilots and retries. Three repeats per cell are a practical minimum, not a statistical power guarantee.
