03 / VISION

Research aims

I want to understand where the model agrees with the robot, where it falls short, and why.

An ideal differential-drive model describes motion through the drive wheels. A real robot also has passive caster reorientation, rolling resistance and floor contact. This study asks when those effects matter. The floor tends to make a tidy model’s life harder.

I will compare the model and robot under the same commands and documented conditions, measuring the tracking benefit and completion-time cost of smoother motion profiles.
