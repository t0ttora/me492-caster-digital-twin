# Arche JANUS · independent navigation-policy audit

27 November 2026 · Release metadata, source inspection and local read-tests

**Test frozen ViNT first for the taught approach to the dock. Use GNM as the cheaper learned baseline and Nav2 to a mapped staging pose followed by classical QR docking as the decision baseline.** ViNT and GNM can consume the same six-frame camera history, goal photograph and route memory, and feed the same waypoint tracker. GNM has the smaller released artifact and fewer published parameters. Neither model has demonstrated a gain on JANUS. For a fixed “return to charger” command, a station-ID lookup already supplies the destination. The narrower reason to spend compute on vision is to test whether a staging-view photograph improves branch selection when odometry is plausible but biased or two approaches look alike. [ViNT architecture][vpaper] · [Berkeley deployment][bnav]

**Withhold model smoke on both hosts.** No matching runtime, cached checkpoint and eligible JANUS route sequence were established together. No package installation, weight-body download, forward pass, GPU reservation, remote job, MATLAB operation, publication or push took place. The checks below independently establish which sources can be read, which artifact headers are public and which parts of the control interface work in isolation.

### Dependencies that affect the choice

- **Berkeley** ships GNM's modified MobileNet and constructs ViNT's EfficientNets with `from_name`. The forward constructors inspected here do not fetch ImageNet weights. The shared loader imports ROS message types, EfficientNet, and a diffusion-policy U-Net even for deterministic models. The reference deployment specifies Python 3.8.5, CUDA toolkit 11.x and Diffusers 0.11.1. The latter has Apache-2.0 terms. The Stanford diffusion-policy code is MIT, EfficientNet-PyTorch is Apache-2.0, and PyTorch/Torchvision carry BSD-style terms and notices. Those source licenses cannot resolve the Drive checkpoints' terms. [ViNT constructor][bV] · [Unified loader][bload] · [Environment][benv] · [Diffusers][diff] · [Diffusion Policy][dp] · [EfficientNet][efficient] · [PyTorch][torch] · [Torchvision][vision]
- **Edge and LeLaN** use OpenAI CLIP ViT-B/32. Edge constructs the complete CLIP model, converts it to float32 and calls its text encoder even in the image-only example. CLIP code and bundled tokenizer are in the MIT project. The loader pins the external checkpoint via the SHA-256 in its URL. Anonymous HEAD returned **353,976,522 B**, giving **788,252,987 B** of Edge + CLIP checkpoint files before runtime allocation. A separate grant for the external CLIP binary was not established in the inspected materials. Match the BPE vocabulary to the exact CLIP source. The Edge demo also imports UTM (MIT). Single-subgoal tensors `[B,18,64,85]` and `[B,3,64,85]`. ViNT uses 512-D tokens and early goal fusion. GNM uses two 1024-D encodings. [Preprocessing][bload] · [ViNT config][bvint] · [GNM config][bgnm] | Distance head predicts sampled time steps. Action head returns `[B,5,4]`. XY is already cumulative in the observation-time local frame. Heading pair is cos/sin of yaw relative to current yaw. Training divides XY by dataset metric spacing × sample stride. Reference deployment multiplies chosen XY by max speed / rate, here 0.2/4 = **0.05 m per unit**, and tracks index 2. At an explicitly matched 4 Hz replay grid, five future samples span 1.25 s. The actual dataset stride and camera timestamps govern time alignment. [Labels][bdata] · [Frame transform][bframe] · [GNM head][bG] · [ViNT head][bV] · [Deployment][bnav] · [Robot config][brobot] |
| **NoMaD** | Current + three past 96×96 RGB frames and a local goal image, goal-mask value 0 for conditioned navigation. | Temporal distance plus eight normalized body-frame XY points per trajectory, with no heading head. Default example uses ten denoising steps, eight samples and sample zero for control. At the declared 4 Hz comparison grid eight future labels span 2 s. Goal ambiguity would have to justify the sampling cost. [Config][bnomad] · [Deployed selection and scale][bnav] |
| **OmniVLA-Edge** | Six 96×96 RGB frames and a current 224×224 view. Mode 6 image goal, 7 language, 9 both. Demo repeats a still and constructs satellite, pose, image and text inputs even when masked. No GPS sensor is needed to supply a masked placeholder for modes 6/7. | Eight cumulative local `x,y,cos yaw,sin yaw` waypoints plus an internal distance head. Eight future intervals at exactly 3 Hz would be 2.67 s. Preserve the eight samples and their label times before assigning a metric-time horizon. | **MBRA** | Released config uses six 96×96 RGB observations and a goal image. **Frame and loader findings.** JANUS uses axle midpoint, x forward, y left, z up and CCW yaw. Camera optical coordinates are right/down/forward. Transform future odometry into the capture-time axle frame and verify camera extrinsics separately. Do not apply a second XY cumulative sum. A lateral waypoint specifies a turn or arc on the differential drive. Berkeley clips forward speed at zero, so the reference tracker cannot carry out a reversing maneuver. Route-node equality in the navigator is an arrival *prediction*. Only the classical controller may accept the station or change charging state. [Training transform][bframe] · [Deployment and arrival][bnav] · [Reference tracker][bpd]

The Berkeley loader expects a serialized object at `checkpoint["model"]` for ViNT/GNM and a state dictionary for NoMaD. All three go through `strict=False`. An importable legacy model class and complete keys/shapes are prerequisites. Require exact trainable-parameter coverage and verify expected buffers, config, finite outputs and tensor axes before scoring. On Edge the policy load is strict, but CPU mode is unresolved: the forward calls `obs_img.get_device()` and uses that integer in mask transfers, with another such branch for mode 9. Its sample's pure-heading branch calls `clip_angle` without an import or definition in that script. LeLaN's paper caches text, while the released callback initializes `flag_once=0` and only sets it in the other branch, so repeated encoding can change timing. These are source-level findings. None establishes an observed model failure on either host. [Berkeley loader][bload] · [Edge loader][eload] · [Edge mask code][enet] · [Edge controller][erun] · [LeLaN callback][lrun]

## Memory, latency and the smoke decision

| Evidence | Scope |
|---|---|
| The ViNT paper gives 31M parameters and cites 8.7M for GNM. At FP32 these imply about **124 MB / 34.8 MB** of parameter storage. CausalNav reports **150 / 110 / 95 ms** mean per cycle for ViNT / GNM / NoMaD, in its real-environment evaluation with i9-13900H and RTX 4070. [ViNT parameters][vpaper] · [Timing, Table IV][causal] | Parameter arithmetic excludes activations, checkpoint unpickling, extra route nodes, allocator and other workloads. Mean cycle time on that host does not establish p95 on JANUS or Elo. |
| AsyncVLA lists OmniVLA-Edge at **6 Hz** onboard Orin and **108M** parameters. The original OmniVLA paper's Table VI gives **50M** for Edge. [AsyncVLA Table I][asyncp] · [OmniVLA Table VI][epaper] | Counting scopes are unresolved. The 6 Hz figure is an operating rate. There is no matching host peak or end-to-end percentile, and the reference runner loads the separate CLIP model. |
| LeLaN reports **54 ms** per action calculation on Orin AGX for its simple single-image evaluation with cached text. FlowNav reports **19.0 ms** at two steps and **48.6 ms** at ten on RTX A500 Laptop; ten-step NoMaD in that study was **44.8 ms**. [LeLaN Table 2 and Appendix G.2][lelp] · [FlowNav compute comparison][flowp] | Neither tests the Elo GPU or JANUS queue, QR decoder, route window or freshness logic. Matched runtime latency for the MBRA short expert is unknown. Peak RSS/VRAM for all shortlisted configurations on our two hosts is unknown. |

**Elo.** Supplied verified state is Windows, RTX 3050 Ti Laptop, 4,096 MiB total and approximately 3,835 MiB free at the reported observation. Driver-reported CUDA 13.4 does not identify an installed inference toolkit or compatible PyTorch build. Default Python is 3.14 with no PyTorch. Berkeley's reference deployment targets 3.8.5 with ROS 1 imports. Edge setup specifies Python 3.10, torch 2.2.0, torchvision 0.17.0. The torch 2.2.0 Windows x64 wheel inventory has cp38–cp312 builds and no cp314 build. A differently configured Windows replay could be feasible, but its ABI, memory headroom and latency remain unknown. This audit did not poll the remote machine or touch its MATLAB work. [Berkeley environment][benv] · [Edge environment][eenv] · [Torch 2.2.0 wheel inventory][torchw]

**Mac.** In the SSD lane, the default interpreter identified Darwin arm64 / Python 3.9.6. A metadata-only module probe found no torch, torchvision, efficientnet_pytorch, diffusers, clip, rospy or onnxruntime there. The work disk is external. No dedicated model backend, complete artifact set or compatible runtime was established. Available disk space or a CPU fallback string is insufficient evidence of usable inference. **There is no bounded, executable weight-backed smoke to recommend under the current constraints.** A future decision can reopen when a matching isolated runtime and verified, already-local weights are available on an idle host.

## Falsifiable matched experiment

**Decision question.** At a prespecified junction or last ambiguous decision *before* QR ownership, does replacing the classical pre-dock proposal with frozen ViNT increase the fraction of episodes with the correct first branch or hold? Measure actual arrival at the correct QR staging envelope only in a subsequent closed-loop comparison. A recorded camera follows the recorded driver after a divergent action.

1. **Freeze the reference task.** Give every arm the same station-ID task, surveyed staging pose/envelope, teach-run reference images mapped to nodes, robot footprint including caster, camera calibration and safety limits. Use a whole scene at an attainable pose as each goal image. Keep normal map/localization/obstacle inputs available to the real Nav2 arm. Record its distro, parameter/map/TF hashes, costmaps or reconstructable sensor streams, planner/controller and original or causally replayed outputs. If these inputs are missing, mark the comparison pending. A Python teacher is a separate arm. No ground-truth pose or future frame enters any policy.
2. **Pair the alternatives.** A = mapped station lookup → Nav2 staging → shared QR controller. B = ViNT before the identical QR controller. C = GNM under the same conditions as B. Include a cheap visual sensitivity check if available: nearest taught keyframe in the same bounded search window with the same tracker. Freeze the window, threshold, tracker, maximum speed, observation budget and handoff supervisor on development data. Use the same node library for ViNT/GNM, and log the node each picks. Include an additional fixed-subgoal pass to isolate action prediction from node selection. The primary system comparison retains the native Nav2 planner and controller.
3. **Capture and split.** Proposed sealed set: 12 held-out route/layout groups × four separately recorded or rendered episodes, 48 paired episodes. Within each group include nominal, illumination/clutter shift, lookalike approach, and bounded localization bias with valid odometry. Allocate other layouts and sessions to development. Teach each test route once in its own earlier session without access to the sealed traversal. Keep overlapping footage, scenario variants and their derived images together. Freeze split, seed, frame hashes, goal library, stratum counts, join limits and criteria before reading predictions. If only repeated variants of one layout exist, report pilot counts and leave the gain undecided.
4. **Join causally and label independently.** Key images by episode and unique sample ID, QR candidates by sample plus detection index, and poses/commands by timestamp and frame. Use monotonic capture and arrival in one clock domain, start a new run after resets, interpolate unwrapped yaw for scoring, and zero-order hold commands. Use only already-arrived observations and commands for inference. Pull future poses solely for labels. For the proposed 4 Hz ViNT/GNM grid, select the latest eligible prior image within 50 ms of each requested time, with distinct frames, or count the episode decision as unavailable. Keep missing/stale data as explicit outcomes. Certify matching scenario/config, initial pose, time, achieved trajectory and command type before transferring per-frame Simscape labels. Otherwise caster/force tags stay at scenario level. Label the correct branch or hold from the surveyed goal, independently registered obstacle geometry and blinded annotation before inspecting **any** arm's actions.
5. **Score the recorded states.** Preselect one decision instant per episode and a marked branch boundary. Classify each arm's local path or bounded command roll-out by the first branch boundary it crosses over the common horizon. Freeze boundary geometry and prediction horizon on development data. Failure to identify a required branch by the deadline counts as incorrect; an explicit timely hold is correct only on a hold-labeled case. Report paired discordant counts and per-stratum success. Also report future-odometry ADE/FDE in m, wrapped yaw error in rad where defined, node error, false advance, disagreement with operator, availability and repeats. Route-trace error is a diagnostic where multiple paths are valid. Estimate clearance only against registered geometry including the swept caster footprint.
6. **Exercise ownership and clocks.** Include Q01–Q03 (center and sign), Q06–Q09 (loss, recovery, wrong/duplicate IDs), Q13 (stale/disordered), Q14 (blocked), Q15/Q16 (separate contact/charge), O03/O04/O05 (crossing, cancel, odometry fault) and M08 (reversal). Log proposed, limited, accepted and executed velocities separately. Keep classical QR ownership latched through loss/reacquisition. To begin dock driving, require the correct fresh ID and ±2% centering for 0.4 s continuously. Once driving, re-center with zero forward speed outside ±4%. Use contract freshness 0.20 s. Fail the arm on any wrong-station handoff, accepted learned motion after handoff, invalid-observation forward drive during docking, or learned charge authorization. Count violations and time to last nonzero command. Physical contact and BMS confirmation require independent signals.
7. **Decide before advancing.** Proposed minimum worthwhile effect is **+10 percentage points** ViNT over Nav2 on the primary replay endpoint, with the lower bound of a paired 95% interval above zero. Use 10,000 bootstrap resamples of whole layouts, retaining their paired episodes, and publish raw counts; 12 groups can leave a wide interval. Prefer GNM if it clears the classical gate and ViNT's incremental benefit over GNM is unresolved. Any failed hard gate stops advancement. Profile batch-one decisions with the frozen goal window, identical timing instrumentation, 20 warm-ups and at least 200 timed ticks plus real-time route playback. Log cold load, absolute/incremental peak RSS, peak allocated/reserved VRAM, p50/p95/p99 capture-to-accepted age, dropped frames and deadline misses including QR processing. Proposed p95 cap is 200 ms or the clearance/braking-derived limit, whichever is shorter. Expired proposals always hold. Timing or quality misses retain the classical baseline.
8. **Confirm the outcome replay cannot measure.** If the screen passes, branch a simulator from identical initial snapshots/seed and dynamic event schedules. Each arm receives images generated by its own actions, the same calibrated QR controller and the same measurement budget. Count episodes that enter the independently surveyed staging envelope within the frozen timeout and obtain fresh correct-ID acquisition with no contact/intervention. Apply the same +10 pp / positive paired lower bound to actual staging success and require zero hard-gate violations. Record handoff latency and approach failures separately. A synthetic pass would justify considering an operator-approved physical trial; physical stopping, contact, charging and caster response remain open.

For a separate language experiment, hold the six observations fixed, pair two valid instructions demanding opposite branches, and compare OmniVLA-Edge modes 7, 6 and 9 to phrase-to-station lookup. Include absent targets and synonyms and count both correct target selection and abstention/coverage. Improvements here apply to tasks in which language changes the goal. They supply no evidence for replacing the fixed-station docking controller.
