# QR observation contract addendum

The quadrilateral center is the intersection of the projected diagonals
of the four ordered corners. The image-space average can shift across an
acceptance band under perspective and is a different estimator. Reject
degenerate quads and ambiguous target observations. A decoded distractor
with a different ID can coexist with a unique valid target. Another
undecoded quad can hide a duplicate target and blocks forward motion.

A fresh frame with zero candidates provides evidence to a bounded search
action. An absent, invalid-timestamp, stale or disordered frame provides
no turn/advance authority for visual docking. The command log must record
the reason behind the first stopped sample. Mechanical alignment and
velocity stay within their declared dwell together. During the synthetic
charge wait, departure from the contact/pose tolerance invalidates charge
confirmation until contact is re-established. Actual contact and charge
require independent observations with timestamps.

The 27 November Python and Gazebo fixtures use different camera intrinsics,
mounts and station placements. The same station ID, normalization and
sign convention do not establish trajectory parity. Each experiment
records its fixture and camera instead of pooling measurements across
different physical geometries.
