I’m studying how passive casters affect mobile robot motion and whether smoother motion profiles help. This is my working record for ME492, shared with my advisor. The core work is a calibrated caster-aware digital twin and a comparison of baseline and smoother motion profiles. A compact motion-error predictor is a secondary objective.
