What I’m building

I’m studying how passive caster wheels affect a mobile robot when it turns, reverses and docks. The Python controller can follow a route and approach a QR-marked dock. I use Simscape to inspect caster forces and Gazebo to test what happens when the camera or other robot messages arrive late, disappear or show the wrong station.
