What I’m testing

A passive caster has to turn before it can roll in a new direction. I’m measuring how much that changes the path of a robot during a turn, a reversal or a dock approach. I run routes in Python, inspect caster forces in Simscape and use Gazebo to test how the controller handles the wrong QR or late or missing messages.
