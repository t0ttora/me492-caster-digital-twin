04 / MANIFESTO

How I work

01

Physical validation

A simulation describes expected behaviour. Establishing physical accuracy requires independent measurements and comparison.

02

Uncertainty

I record missing access, unsuccessful experiments and model limitations so they can be considered alongside the results.

03

Model complexity

I add a more complex model or learning method when it addresses a measured need. Extra complexity should have a job to do.

04

Reproducibility

Every result should be traceable to its conditions, parameters, recordings and decisions. Final test data stay out of tuning.
