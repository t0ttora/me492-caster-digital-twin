Arche · Project and author

About

Arche is an individual Mechanical Engineering project by Oluş Emre Demir at Yeditepe University.

Project

The ME492 study joins mechanical modelling, controls and robot experiments to measure the effect of passive casters on a differential-drive platform. This journal holds the methods, simulation evidence and materials needed to reproduce the work.

Term
Fall 2026 · 30 September 2026 – 14 January 2027
Research scope
Caster dynamics, trajectory tracking, QR docking and simulation-to-robot comparison.
