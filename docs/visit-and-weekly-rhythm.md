# Visit Follow Through and Weekly Execution

Decisions actions and advisor visibility

## Before and after the visit

By 2 October, prepare the deck and ask the advisor to coordinate the 6/7 October visit. Two working days before the confirmed meeting, provide the short agenda and request that Arche’s technical contact attend. During the meeting, the student records decisions while the advisor confirms academic boundaries. No firm commitment is assumed without a named owner.

Within one working day after the visit, prepare a written record of platform revision, granted/restricted access, file and log delivery dates, test slots, instrumentation, publication rules, costs and open questions. The advisor and Arche contact confirm or correct the record. Within three working days, test the supplied files/sample log and update only assumptions affected by evidence. Retain the academic deadlines.

| Action | Owner | Due or evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visit coordination | Advisor with student support | Target 6/7 Oct, fallback 13/14 Oct. |
| CAD and interface sample | Arche named technical contact | Request within 3 working days after visit. |
| Usable logging pilot | Student and Arche operator | Target 20/21 Oct. G2 review 23 Oct. |
| Test and data permissions | Arche and advisor | By G1 16 Oct or recorded restricted route. |
| Academic reduced-scope decision | Advisor and student | By 9 Nov if G3 lacks physical evidence. |

## Weekly operating rhythm

Monday: select one demonstrable weekly output and check dependencies. Tuesday/Wednesday: prioritize confirmed visits or experiments; otherwise model. Friday: publish a short GitHub progress record with the planned output, actual work, evidence link, hours, blocker, next output and decision request. Target a 15–20 minute advisor review weekly, subject to availability. Distinguish prepared, executed and independently verified work. Publish company material only within confirmed permissions.

## Advisor briefing - first three to four minutes

“I have prepared the semester execution baseline: scope, experiments, Arche access needs, decision gates and deadlines in one plan. It also states how the academic scope changes if company access is delayed. The 252 hours cover committed work; 32 hours are a risk reserve, not feature development.”

Show page 1 for objectives/scope, pages 3–4 for the timetable, then page 5 for risk and fallback decisions. Explain that the remaining pages contain the experiment protocol, secondary learning task and Arche access checklist. Use the public GitHub project home for progress, evidence, decisions and current reports between meetings.
