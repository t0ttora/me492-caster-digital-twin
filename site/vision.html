Research questions

An ideal differential-drive model gets its motion from the two drive wheels. A passive caster takes time to turn into the new direction, especially after a reversal. I want to measure how much that changes the path and whether smoother commands help.

Does the caster model predict motion more accurately?

I’ll send the same commands to the ideal and caster-aware models, then compare both predictions with an independent recording of the robot’s pose. The Python and Simscape replays show how the models respond in simulation. The pilot will establish the physical reference.

Do smoother commands improve tracking?

Python already compares baseline and smoother profiles on synthetic surfaces. The robot study will use repeated confirmation runs to compare position error, yaw error and completion time.

Can telemetry predict yaw error?

This secondary study compares a compact short-horizon predictor with persistence and a simple numerical baseline. Whole runs stay in separate data splits. The ViNT check belongs to the docking study. The telemetry predictor still needs a suitable dataset.

The physical plan covers one robot configuration, two approved surfaces and four maneuver families. It requests 24 calibration runs and 48 confirmation runs. Three repetitions per condition are the starting target.
