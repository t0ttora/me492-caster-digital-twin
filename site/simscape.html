October · Simulink baselines

From ideal motion to coupled dynamics

M0 integrates ideal differential-drive motion from wheel commands. M2 replaces the ideal drive response with separate axle torques, coupled body and wheel inertia, passive caster dynamics, friction and support loads.

M0 passed 32 Windows checks across six synthetic cases. The M2 v1 MATLAB run covered 12 scenarios and passed 60 numerical and 13 workflow checks.

November · Contact instrumentation

Building V5 and instrumenting V6

V5 refined the contact cylinders. V6 added measurement channels for axle pose, caster angle, wheel motion and individual normal loads. The later JANUS runs also recorded contact penetration and axle effort. The saved V6 model and the reconstructed +30° alignment experiment are separate files.

The reconstructed experiment applies 0.01 N·m to each driven wheel from a +30° caster start. Its ode4 solver runs at 25 µs and the export samples every 100 µs, yielding 30,001 rows over 3 s.

27 November · Native baseline matrix

Measuring solver sensitivity

We crossed three initial caster angles (−30°, 0°, +30°) with 50, 25 and 12.5 µs fixed steps in MATLAB R2026b. Each run used the same three-second effort input. The finer +30° follow-up used 6.25 µs.

At 6.25 µs the maximum difference against 12.5 µs was still 0.002633 rad/s. The solver study establishes a remaining sensitivity in this fixture.

JANUS · Projected observations

Revising the QR docking controller

The native plant supplies measured pose and torque. The controller receives ideal projected QR corners. Four early V4 runs stopped beyond the 265 mm depth limit. Revised runs held depth and mechanical pose bounds together for 0.40 s.

In the persistent-loss qualification, the command fell to zero at 1.060 s, the measured stop dwell completed at 1.417 s and the abort latched at 2.060 s. Physical dock contact and BMS confirmation require separate measurements.

Cross-platform diagnostic

Replaying the same Python torques

Eight native open-loop runs replayed both logged Python axle torque channels. The match was checked on the native logging grids. Two replays were repeated when the verified Python QR streams changed.
